## Implementation notes

### VZ-42 car skin
- `src/render/skins/car.ts` registers `car.body` (box) and `car.wheel` (cylinder, `carWheel.ts`); `carKit.ts` holds the ref-counted shared geometry/material lease and merge batch. The shell is a lofted-by-extrusion body (centre tub + two wheel-arched side pieces, tumblehome/taper baked in, vertex-colour underbody darkening), tinted glass, roof/pillars, mirrors, grille, bumpers, interior (seats, dash, steering wheel, emissive cluster), headlights (emissive, always on) and tail-lights (dim glow; bright while `KeyS` is held, smoothed). About 42k triangles for the whole car.
- Suspension: for every body tagged `car-wheel` within 2.5 m, the chassis skin expresses the wheel's world pose in the chassis frame and stretches damper rod/tube, coil spring and two lower arms from the fixed mount to the hub (real slider travel); brake calipers follow hub position and steering yaw but not the spin; brake discs spin with the wheel skin.
- `buildCar`: chassis `skin: car.body` + tag `car-body`; wheels `skin: car.wheel` + tag `car-wheel`; the glass Cabin is `hidden` (still simulated). Paint follows the chassis' live `material.color` because the skin reads it each frame through `bodiesWithTag('car-body')`, so **colour edits need no hook change**. `skin.params.color` pins a colour instead.
- Inspector: `src/ui/extensions/skin.ts` ("Skin" section, order 160) lists registered skins that fit the shape, plus None; undoable through `ctx.edit`.
- Known limits: skin geometry is fixed-size (resizing the chassis box does not scale the shell); the car is left-hand drive (driver at -Z); the wheel's outer face is chosen from the authored z sign of the wheel body.

### VZ-23 weather
* **Document**: optional `settings.weather` `{ kind none|rain|snow|leaves|dust, intensity 0-1, lightning, wet 0-1, windSpeed 0-40 m/s, windDir deg }`, sanitised in `src/model/sanitize/weather.ts` (absent stays absent; junk repairs to clear). No schema bump (optional field). Edited through `store.updateSettings` (undoable) from the World tab's Weather section and the palette commands `Weather: rain / snow / leaves / dust / clear`.
* **Wind**: the repo had no scene wind (the effects package's `env.wind` was never set), so the weather block owns it and `applyWeather` (`src/app/weatherSync.ts`) pushes it into `fx.setEnvironment({ wind })`: particles, sparks, smoke and dust all drift with the same vector. Rain follows 70 % of it, the rest 100 % (`windDrift`).
* **Rendering**: `src/render/weather.ts`. One `InstancedBufferGeometry` quad per kind with a per-instance random seed; position, wrap-around in a box that follows the camera/target, fall, wind drift, sway, tumbling (leaves) and streak tilt (rain) are all computed in the vertex shader from a time uniform; `instanceCount` is the only per-frame CPU value (no particle loop). Cap per tier (low/medium/high/ultra = 1500/6000/12000/20000 x kind factor rain 1, snow 0.8, dust 0.5, leaves 0.12) scaled by intensity. Buffers rebuild only when kind or tier changes; geometry/material/DOM overlay are disposed.
* **Lightning**: seeded `LightningScheduler` (scene seed; heavier weather strikes more often). A flash brightens the particles via a uniform and raises the opacity of a DOM overlay on the viewport (capped at 0.5). It deliberately does not touch the post stack and adds no scene light (a new light would recompile every material). "Reduce motion" scales the overlay to 25 %. A "Strike" button previews it.
* **Wet look**: roughness x (1 - 0.78 wet) and a slightly darker colour (ground 42 %, other surfaces 12 % at full wet) on the ground slab and every MeshStandardMaterial under the bodies group, swept about every 0.75 s; the original values are remembered, a re-assigned material (document edit) becomes the new original, and everything is restored when the weather stops. The document is never edited. Rain wets by itself (0.3 + 0.7 intensity).
* **Verified**: unit tests (sanitiser, round trip, tier budget, wind mapping, lightning determinism) and the e2e file above. Not done: splashes/ripples on the ground, puddle reflections (only roughness/colour change; no screen-space reflections), rain/snow occlusion under roofs, thunder sound.
