## Implementation notes (JT-B)
- **Load estimation** (`src/physics/world/jointLoad.ts`): Newton–Euler momentum balance on the part of the joint graph beyond a joint; exact for bridges with a free (unsupported) side, shared-load heuristic otherwise (documented in the file header and in docs/LIMITATIONS.md). Runs only while a joint is breakable or something asked for loads (`SimOp watchLoads`: the Joint-stress overlay and the open joint inspector). Joint buffer stride is now 9 (`J.load/torque/ratio/damage/exact`).
- **Breakage**: `JointEntity.breakage?` (`src/model/breakage.ts`), sanitised, inspector section for every joint type but springs (those keep `spring.breakForce`). `jointBroken` events carry `torque` for load breaks. Broken joints leave the live world; a rebuilt world (Reset) restores them.
- **Layers**: `SceneSettings.layers?` (names, symmetric matrix, trigger bits); the quadratic drag measures velocity against the weather block's scene wind (`weatherWind`); `BodyEntity.oneWay?` (local solid-side direction). New UI lives in `src/ui/collisionLayersSection.ts` (World tab), `src/ui/inspector/bodyJt.ts` (registered from `src/ui/extensions/bodyPhysicsB.ts`) and `src/styles/jointsB.css`.
- Other packages: any new joint type gets load estimation and the breakage section automatically (edges of the joint graph = every non-spring joint that has an engine joint). Types that apply their own impulses outside the engine (like JT-A's couplings) are captured through Δv, but their wrench is only the shared-load estimate.

## Implementation notes (JT-A)

* **Example content (JT-1..JT-8).** `src/model/content/joints-mechanisms.ts` registers ten scenes in the *Mechanisms* category (`jt-atwood`, `jt-gear-train`, `jt-belt-drive`, `jt-rack-pinion`, `jt-screw-jack`, `jt-crane-winch`, `jt-ragdoll-arms`, `jt-sixdof-platform`, `jt-cardan-shaft`, `jt-soft-diving-board`) and three prefabs (`jt-gear-pair`, `jt-ragdoll-arm`, `jt-soft-board`). Each runs a motor or a falling load as soon as Play is pressed. `tests/jointExamples.test.ts` simulates every scene headless (the checks named in the ticks above) and round-trips each through `serializeDocument` → `parseDocument` with no warning and identical `params`; `tests/jointParamsSanitize.test.ts` fuzzes `sanitizeParams` and loads params-less (old) joints.
* **Design rules the examples follow** (they are properties of the one-pass coupling rows, see `docs/LIMITATIONS.md` › "Joint types without an engine joint"): coupling joints join bodies that each have their own hinge / slider to the world; a load is *part of* the coupled body (the screw jack's crate is a compound part) or hangs from the coupling (winch), never merely rests on it; a pinned driver of a screw is heavy (a real steel screw is 1.1 t); chains of soft welds are kept to two.
* **Known defects reported to the lead, not hidden**: the gravity-loaded screw jack climbs at about 70 % of pitch × turns (exact without gravity / across the axis); a chain of soft welds sags more than analytic (≈ 1.5× for two, ≈ 5× for five); the gear / belt / rack / screw `force` readout is the pre-step impulse only, so it reads ≈ 0 for a load applied by the engine (the torque trade-off is therefore evidenced through the stall torque, not the readout); the winch `radiusA` (spool radius) is not used by the physics (the motor's target velocity is metres of rope per second); a cone-twist limit overshoots up to 0.3 rad at the end of a three-link chain (0.02 rad for a single limb), so the ragdoll's wrists are limited hinges.
